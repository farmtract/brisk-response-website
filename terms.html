Reason: These incidents are urgent and have the potential to escalate rapidly, necessitating a quick but slightly longer response time than the most critical incidents.

Quartile 3: High Priority Response (16-30 minutes) Incidents: Burglary, Assault, Kidnapping, Domestic Violence, Sexual Assault, Stalking, Wanted Person, Death Threats, Vehicle Collision, Pedestrian Accident, Hit and Run, Road Hazard, Illegal Wildlife Trade, Human Trafficking, Organ Trafficking, Repatriation Concerns, Health Emergency (OFWs), Missing Person (OFWs) Reason: These incidents are serious and require timely intervention, but they are less immediate in threat compared to those in Quartile 1 and 2.

Quartile 4: Standard Response (31-60 minutes) Incidents: Fraud, Money Laundering, Drug-Related, Vandalism, Trespassing, Public Intoxication, Disorderly Conduct, Noise Complaint, Protests, Riots, Employment Issues (OFWs), Unpaid Wages (OFWs), Legal Assistance (OFWs), Over Pricing, Neglect of Senior Citizen Discount, Neglect of PWD Discount, False Advertising, Substandard Goods, Refund Issues, Service Denial, Unfair Business Practices, Misleading Pricing, Warranty Violations. Reason: These incidents are important and should be addressed promptly, but they do not pose an immediate threat to life or safety.
